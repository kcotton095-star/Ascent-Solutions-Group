South Carolina HVAC answering service FAQ

Do you answer emergency HVAC calls after hours in South Carolina?

Yes. Ascent answers nights, weekends, and holidays, captures the caller's name, number, address, and issue, and flags true HVAC emergencies — no heat in freezing weather, no cooling in extreme heat, and anything involving a gas smell or a carbon monoxide alarm — then texts and emails you the details right away.

What happens if a caller smells gas or a CO alarm is going off?

Safety comes first. The caller is told to hang up and call 911 immediately, and to call their gas company once they are safe. We still log the call, mark it as an emergency, and alert you right away.
