South Carolina electrician answering service FAQ

Do you answer emergency electrical calls after hours in South Carolina?

Yes. Ascent answers nights, weekends, and holidays, captures the caller's name, number, address, and issue, and flags true electrical emergencies — sparking or arcing, burning smells, a hot or buzzing panel, exposed live wiring, or a power loss that isn't a utility outage — then texts and emails you the details right away.

What happens if a caller reports sparking or a burning smell?

Safety comes first. If there is any smoke or flame, the caller is told to hang up and call 911 immediately. If it is safe, they are advised that switching off the main breaker can help prevent a fire. The call is still logged as an emergency and you are alerted right away.

Will callers know they've reached an answering service?

No. Ascent answers in your electrical company's name and sounds like a professional receptionist on your team, in English or Spanish, so your customers feel taken care of from the first ring.
