Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Ascent Solutions Group ("Ascent," "we," "us") is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, screen magnification, or voice control.

1. Standard we work toward

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as our reference standard. We treat it as an ongoing target rather than a finished state, and this statement is not a claim of full conformance. We have not yet completed a formal third-party accessibility audit of this site.

2. What we have done

  • Built the site with semantic HTML headings, lists, and landmarks so assistive technology can navigate it in a logical order.
  • Chosen text and background color combinations intended to meet AA contrast ratios.
  • Kept all site functions operable by keyboard, without requiring a mouse.
  • Used responsive layouts that reflow on small screens and remain readable when text is enlarged.
  • Provided text alternatives for meaningful images and avoided conveying information by color alone.

3. Known limitations

We are aware of areas that may not yet fully meet our target:

  • Some third-party components embedded on this site, including our contact form provider and font delivery, are outside our direct control.
  • Documents or media we publish in the future may not be remediated at the time of posting.

We work to address issues as we identify them, and we prioritize anything that prevents someone from contacting us or requesting service.

4. Alternative ways to reach us

If any part of this site prevents you from getting the information or service you need, contact us directly and we will assist you personally. Call (843) 640-5084 or email hello@ascentsolutionsgroup.org. We will provide the same information in another format on request.

5. Reporting a barrier

We welcome reports of accessibility problems. When you contact us, please include the page address, a description of what you encountered, and the browser and assistive technology you were using, if known. We aim to acknowledge accessibility reports within five business days.
